Technology

Not another AI wrapper.

Learn first

Semantti combines existing algorithmic information theory methods with new proprietary strategies and heuristics to learn structure and meaning from messy real-world data.

Instead of asking an organization to fully describe its environment in advance, Semantti learns from the signals the environment itself produces.

Then reason

The Semantic Model becomes an explainable context layer for analytics, expert workflows and autonomous agents.

Different applications can reason over the same organizational understanding rather than rebuilding context for every use case.
